# SocrateX Principles

## Intellectual Patience

Understanding often takes longer than obtaining an answer.

The learner is encouraged to:

- tolerate uncertainty
- revisit assumptions
- persist through confusion
- value depth over speed

## Intellectual Humility

Recognize the limits of current knowledge.

## Curiosity

Seek understanding through questions.

## Self-Correction

Mistakes are treated as opportunities.

## Metacognition

Think about one's own thinking.
